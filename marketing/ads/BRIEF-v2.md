## Price claim
With THB 399 and 699 the first set's headline no longer holds: Stock Analysis Pro is THB 327. The v2 headline is "Both LEKHA plans cost less than 9 of the 10 platforms shown". The build script stops with an error if that count stops being true, so a price or competitor change cannot silently leave a false headline. The twelve-month chart (D3) draws Stock Analysis Pro, which is cheaper than LEKHA Paid, and states that in its footnote.

Competitor prices and sources are the table in `BRIEF.md`, checked 23 Nov 2026 and to be re-checked on the day ads go live. Annual billing makes several competitors cheaper than shown; LEKHA bills monthly only.
